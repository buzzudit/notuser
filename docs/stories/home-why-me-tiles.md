# Home Why me tile cleanup

## Summary
Tighten the homepage Why section so it presents three concrete reasons to consider Udit instead of a broader set of overlapping positioning tiles.

## Problem
The previous section had six tiles and some repeated abstract claims. It made the section feel longer than needed and less concrete for hiring or leadership audiences scanning the homepage.

## Desired outcome
The homepage shows a concise Why me section with exactly three tiles grounded in current scope, enterprise platform experience, and technical fluency.

## In scope
- Rename the section eyebrow to "Why me".
- Reduce the section to three tiles.
- Rewrite section copy and tile copy to be more concrete.
- Preserve the existing section layout and card styling.

## Out of scope
- Redesigning the homepage.
- Changing case studies, leadership model content, AI briefing behavior, or navigation.
- Changing visual language beyond the existing content-driven layout adjustment.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The homepage Why section eyebrow reads "Why me".
2. The section renders exactly three tiles.
3. Tile copy is concrete and avoids overlapping abstract claims.
4. Existing responsive layout and styling patterns are preserved.
5. `lint` and `build` pass.

## Definition of done
- Homepage section copy is updated.
- `homeWhyUdit` contains exactly three items.
- Story documentation is added.
- Local `lint` and `build` pass.
